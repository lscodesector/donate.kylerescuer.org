Frequently asked questions
Is my donation secure?
Yes. Payments are processed securely through PayPal, one of the most trusted payment platforms in the world, with bank-level encryption. No card or banking data is ever stored on our site.
Is there a minimum amount?
Any amount is welcome. Every bit of help keeps the more than 400 lives that depend on Kyle going.
Where exactly does the money go?
Donations are directed toward direct animal care, things like food, rescues, veterinary treatment, medication, and shelter support. Kyle posts regular updates on Instagram.
Who is SOS Animal Help?
An animal protection organization that supports independent rescuers like Kyle. It receives the donations and distributes them with full traceability.
Will I get a receipt for my donation?
Yes. PayPal automatically emails you a receipt as soon as your donation is processed.
